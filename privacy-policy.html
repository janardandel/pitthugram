Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Pitthugram ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of our users, including coaching institute owners, teachers, and students. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our platform services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you register for a free setup, request a trial, create an account, or communicate with our support team:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and coaching institute name.
  • Account & Academic Data: Student rosters, batch configurations, exam schedules, and MCQ assessments created or administered through our platform.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction logs collected for security and operational integrity.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following business purposes:

  • To provision and administer your coaching institute's learning portal and assessment tools.
  • To deliver login credentials, transactional notices, and system alerts via email and WhatsApp.
  • To prevent fraud, automated abuse, and spam through verification tools (including Cloudflare Turnstile).
  • To provide customer support and technical troubleshooting.

4. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting, database, and messaging partners) strictly necessary to deliver our services, and when required by law.
